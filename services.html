Process

From first call to launch

  1. Book a call

    Tell us about the client, the goal and the deadline. We recommend a package or scope a custom one.

  2. Agree scope and pay the deposit

    You get a written scope covering pages, content, revisions and exclusions. Work starts after the 50% deposit.

  3. Build and review

    We design or develop to the agreed scope and share progress for review within your revision rounds.

  4. Launch and hand over

    We deliver the finished site, pay out the balance on delivery and hand over what your team needs to manage it.

FAQ

Common questions

How much does a website cost?

A 4-page starter WordPress website is $250–$500. A Framer website built from an approved design is $600. A custom WordPress website is $1,500–$3,500 depending on scope. A UI/UX audit is $250.

How long does delivery take?

Starter websites take 3–5 business days, Framer websites 5–7 business days and custom WordPress websites 2–4 weeks. Timelines start once scope, content and the deposit are in place.

Do you design the Framer website too?

The $600 Framer package covers development from a design you provide. If you need original design and development, it is scoped and priced higher. Book a call and we will quote it.

What is not included in the prices?

Hosting, domains, paid themes, Framer subscriptions and third-party tools are extra. For the custom WordPress package, e-commerce, complex integrations, copywriting and ongoing maintenance are quoted separately.

How does payment work?

We agree scope, page count, revisions and exclusions in writing before work begins. The standard starting policy is 50% upfront with the balance due at delivery.

Does the UI/UX audit include a redesign?

No. The audit covers one website or product with two 60-minute sessions and a written summary with an action plan. Redesign and implementation are separate work.
